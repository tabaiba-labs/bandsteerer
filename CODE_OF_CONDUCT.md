# Code of conduct

BandSteerer is committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for everyone who uses or contributes to the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on the project’s users.
- Protect other people’s privacy, especially in logs and network diagnostics.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing someone else’s private information, and personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these standards. Proportionate responses can include a private warning, temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository host’s private reporting feature and put **Conduct report** in the title. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. If private reporting is unavailable, open a public issue containing no incident details and ask a maintainer to establish a private channel.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as the circumstances allow. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is itself a violation.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing the BandSteerer project.
